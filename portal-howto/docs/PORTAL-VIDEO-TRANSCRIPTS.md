## directpay

When to report. A direct payment occurs when a debtor pays your organization directly rather than through Southwest Recovery Services. When this happens, it's important to report it through the portal as soon as possible, so SWRS can update the account balance, close the account if it's been paid in full, and keep collections accurately reconciled. Reporting promptly matters. Delays can result in a debtor being contacted for a balance that's already been paid, which reflects poorly on your organization. So when should you report a direct payment? Anytime a debtor mails a check to your office, pays in person, pays through your own payment portal, or when an insurance provider pays you directly. You should also report partial payments and any settlements you accept directly. What you don't need to report, any payment that was made through the SWRS portal, collected by SWRS on your behalf, or that already appears in the recent payments panel on your dashboard. If you're ever unsure, check with your account manager before submitting. Write-offs and attaching documentation. If a debtor has settled their account for less than the full balance owed, you'll need to use the write-off amount field. This represents the portion of the debt your organization is agreeing to forgive as part of the settlement. For example, if a debtor owes five hundred dollars, negotiates a settlement, and pays three hundred and fifty dollars, you would enter three hundred and fifty as the payment amount and one hundred and fifty as the write-off amount. This allows SWRS to close the account correctly and reconcile the full original balance. If you're not sure whether a write-off applies, leave the field blank and contact your account manager before submitting. On the documentation side, attaching a file is optional but strongly recommended. It speeds up processing and reduces the chance of follow-up questions from the SWRS team. Accepted formats include PDF, JPG, PNG, Word documents, and Excel files, up to twenty MB each. Good things to attach include a remittance slip, a copy of the check front, a bank deposit confirmation, or a settlement agreement. When in doubt, attach it.

## fileupload

Uploading files. The Secure File Exchange gives you a safe, encrypted way to send files to Southwest Recovery Services and receive documents back. No email required. All transfers go through the portal, which is especially important for any files containing debtor records, account numbers, or other sensitive information. To send a file, click the Upload New Debtors button in the action bar at the top of your dashboard. Despite the name, this tool accepts any file type, placement data, invoices, contracts, or supporting documents. In the upload dialog, start by entering a file description to help the SWRS processing team identify what you've sent. Something like April twenty twenty-six new placements or invoice ten forty-two supporting docs works great. Next, click Choose Files and select one or more files from your computer. Hold Control on Windows or Command on a Mac to select multiple files at once. When you're ready, click Send File and wait for the success confirmation before closing the dialog. Once the upload is complete, you'll see a confirmation message showing how many files were sent. The dialog stays open so you can send additional files without reopening it. The SWRS processing team is notified automatically. You don't need to call or email to confirm receipt.

## login-reset1

If you've forgotten your password, click the Forgot Password link on the login page. This opens the reset request form. Enter the email address registered to your portal account and click Send Confirmation Email. If an account exists for that address, you'll receive the first email in the reset sequence within a few minutes. Check your inbox for a message from portals@scwrecovery.com with the subject line "Confirm Your Password Reset Request." Open that email and click the Confirm Password Reset link. This verifies that you made the request and opens a confirmation page in your browser. On that confirmation page, click the Confirm Password Reset button to complete the verification step. The portal will then send you a second email with your actual reset link. If you didn't request a reset, simply ignore the email. Your password won't be changed.

## login-reset2

After clicking Confirm on the portal page, a second email will arrive from portals@srecovery.com with the subject line "Reset your password." Open that email and click the Reset Password link. This takes you to the New Password form. Complete this step promptly. The link expires shortly after it's sent. On the form, enter your new password. It must be at least eight characters. Re-enter it in the confirm field, then click Set New Password. That's it. You can now log in at swrsportal.com with your new credentials. One helpful tip, if either email doesn't show up within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder. Both emails come from portals@srecovery.com, so adding that address to your safe senders list will prevent future filtering.

## login-signin

To access the Southwest Recovery Services client portal, open your browser and navigate to swrsportal.com. On the login screen, enter the username assigned to your portal account, then enter your password. Keep in mind that passwords are case-sensitive. When you're ready, click Login. You'll be taken directly to your dashboard. If this is your first time logging in and you haven't received your initial credentials, reach out to your account manager and they'll get you set up. If your account becomes locked after multiple failed login attempts, contact the SWRS support team at two one four three eight seven eight oh six eight or email info@swrecovery.com to have it unlocked.

## mfa

The My Profile page is where you manage your personal portal settings. To access it, click My Profile in the left sidebar of your dashboard. On this page, you'll see your username and email address at the top. These are the credentials registered to your portal account. If either of these needs to be updated, reach out to your account manager. Below that, you'll find two key sections. The multi-factor authentication section lets you enable or disable email verification for your logins, and the Change Password section lets you update your portal password at any time. At the top of the page, the Back to Home link will return you to your dashboard without making any changes.

## profile2

Multi-Factor Authentication or MFA adds a second layer of security to your login. When enabled, you'll receive a six-digit verification code by email each time you sign in. We strongly recommend enabling it on all portal accounts. To get started, check the Enable MFA checkbox on the My Profile page. The setup panel will expand, showing the email address where your codes will be sent. Click Send Code. You'll be redirected to the verification page, and a six-digit code will arrive in your inbox from portals@swrecovery.com. Enter that code and click Verify. The code expires in ten minutes, so enter it promptly. If it expires before you get to it, just click Resend Code to get a fresh one. Once verified, you'll be returned to My Profile and a green confirmation panel will confirm that MFA is now active on your account.

## report1

The Debtor Activity Report lets you view all notes and actions recorded against your accounts for any date range you choose. To get started, click View Activity Report in the left sidebar of your portal dashboard. This opens the filter dialog. You have two ways to set your date range. The quick select buttons This Week, This Month, and This Year automatically fill in the From and To fields for you. Or if you need a specific window, just enter your own custom From and To dates manually. The report supports ranges up to three hundred and sixty-six days and will prompt you to confirm anything longer than a year. You can also narrow your results to a specific debtor. Set the Search By field to Last Name, First Name, or Account Number. Then type a value in the Contains field. If you wanna see your full portfolio, just leave both fields as. When you're ready, click View Report. Your results will load in the same window.

## report2

Once your report loads, you'll see every recorded note and action for your accounts within the selected date range, sorted alphabetically by debtor last name. At the top of the page, the report header shows the date range the report was run for, along with a back link so you can return to the filter dialog without losing your settings. Your account name is displayed to confirm which client account you're viewing. Each row in the report is a single activity record. It shows the debtor name, account number, the date and time of the activity, and a note describing what happened, such as an email sent, a workflow triggered, or a payment posted. You'll notice that some debtors appear more than once, and that's completely normal. Each row represents a separate activity. For example, one row for the direct email sent to the debtor and another for the automated workflow it triggered. Both are captured for audit purposes.

## report3

To export your report, click the Download CSV button in the top right corner of the results page. Your browser will download a file named for the report date range. The file is plain CSV, so you can open it directly in Excel or import it into any reporting tool you use. The columns included are client, first name, last name, account number, notes, date, and rep. One important thing to know, the on-screen view is limited to twenty thousand rows, but the CSV export has no such limit. It will include every matching record regardless of volume. So for large date ranges or full portfolio reports, always use the CSV download to make sure you have the complete data set. If you run into any issues, your report shows no results, or you're not sure about your date range, reach out to your account manager. We're here Monday through Friday, eight AM to five PM Central.

## status1

The Debtor Status screen lets you look up the current standing of any account in your portfolio. You can search for a single debtor by account number or name, or run a broader report filtered by placement date or payment date range. Results are displayed in an interactive grid with expandable payment and promise history. Step one, search for a debtor. The search dialogue gives you two ways to find accounts, by account name for a single record or by date range for multiple records. These two modes cannot be combined. Searching by account number or name. Type at least two characters into the Account or Debtor Name field. The portal will suggest matching records as you type. Selecting a suggestion fills both fields automatically. When searching by date range, select Placement Date Range or Payment Date Range from the Date Filter dropdown. The Account and Name fields will clear automatically. Date range and account name search cannot be used together.

## status2

Reading the results grid. Search results display in a grid showing key financial and status information for each debtor. Each row also has action icons that open additional detail. One, the report header shows the date range and filter type used. Click Back to return to the portal dashboard. Two, the column headers show you debtor name, account hash, current debt, original debt, opened, paid to date, attempts, status, actions. Three, status, color-coded for quick scanning. Green, paid in full. The full original balance has been collected. Brown, settled in full. Account closed at a negotiated amount less than the original balance. Red, flagged. Account has been flagged for special status. Contact your account manager for details. Gray, other. Various other statuses may appear depending on account circumstances. Four, action icons. Each row shows up to three icons depending on what data is available. Press Continue for details.

## status3

Using the action icons. Each row in the results grid can have up to three action icons in the actions column. Icons only appear when the relevant data exists for that debtor. Person icon opens the debtor profile pop-up showing full contact details, address, phone, email, assigned amount, and account dates. Dollar icon expands payment history in line under the debtor row showing post date, amount, discount, reference, balance, and payment type. P icon expands promise history in-line under the debtor row showing promises to pay, including dates, amounts, delivery method, and whether the promise was kept or broken.

## upload1

The portal gives you two ways to submit debtor information to Southwest Recovery Services, and choosing the right one depends on what you're trying to do. Upload New Debtors is for submitting multiple new accounts at once using a formatted spreadsheet. It accepts Excel and CSV files, supports multiple files per session, and replaces the need to email placement files to your rep. Update Current Debtors is for sending a correction note or supporting document for an account that's already in the system. You search by account number or debtor name, add your notes, and the update routes directly to your representative. Before submitting new placements, you'll need the right template. Click Download File Templates in the left sidebar to open the template library. There are two options. The general New Debtors template is an Excel spreadsheet where each row represents one debtor. Use this for regular batch placements. The Single Debtor template is a Word document for one account that requires more detail than the spreadsheet allows. Fill in your template completely before uploading. A few things to have ready for each account: the debtor's full legal name, last known address, original account number and balance, date of last payment or date of service, contact information if available, and any special handling instructions. Once your template is complete, head to the next section to upload it through the portal.

## upload2

Once your placement template is filled out and ready, click Upload New Debtors in the action bar at the top of your dashboard. In the upload dialog, start with a clear file description. Something like, "May 2026 new placements, 42 accounts," helps the processing team triage your file quickly. Click Choose Files and select your completed template. If you have multiple files to submit, hold Control on Windows or Command on a Mac to select them all at once. When you're ready, click Send File and wait for the success confirmation before closing the dialog. The SWRS team is notified automatically. No need to call or email to confirm receipt. A few things to keep in mind before you upload: use the official SWRS template and don't add, remove, or reorder any columns. One row per debtor, no merge cells or summary rows. And make sure each file has a unique name, as the portal won't accept two files with the same filename. For very large batches or accounts with special handling requirements, check with your account manager before uploading to confirm the correct process.

## upload3

Updating an existing debtor account. If you need to send a correction note or supporting document for an account already in the system, click Update Current Debtors in your action bar. The update form has a few required fields. Enter the account number. The field supports autocomplete, so start typing and matching accounts from your portfolio will appear as suggestions. You can also search by debtor last name or company name. Selecting a suggestion will automatically fill in the account number for you. Enter your name so the SWRS team knows who submitted the update and your email address in case they have follow-up questions. In the additional information field, describe what has changed or what action you need taken. Be as specific as possible. This goes directly to your representative. If you have a supporting document to include, use the optional Attach a File field to add one assigned agreement, corrected invoice, or any other relevant document. Once submitted, the update routes directly to your rep. You don't need to follow up separately.

## vault

Downloading files from the vault. When Southwest Recovery Services has a report or document ready for your account, a Secure Document Vault button will appear in your action bar. This button is only visible when files are actually available. If you don't see it, there are simply no documents waiting for you at this time. Click Secure Document Vault to open the client library. All documents currently available for your account will be listed here, typically named with a description and timestamp. Click Download next to any file to save it to your computer. Each file has its own download button so you can grab just what you need. One important thing to keep in mind, download links are active for thirty minutes from the time you open the vault. If a link expires before you download, just close the vault and reopen it to generate fresh links. If you're expecting a document that isn't listed, reach out to your account manager at info@swrecovery.com or call two one four three eight seven eight zero six eight.
