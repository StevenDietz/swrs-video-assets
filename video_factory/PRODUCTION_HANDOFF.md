## Executive Summary — Business Problem
An activation gap after the sales process. Illustrative: of ~100 new signed clients,
~70 submit accounts within 60 days and ~30 never submit a first placement. This project
converts existing signed clients into active placing clients — not a customer-acquisition
campaign. Success = more first placements, fewer inactive clients.

## Primary Objective
Increase the percentage of new clients who submit their first accounts by reducing
friction, uncertainty, and procrastination during the first 60 days after contract execution.

## Core Strategy — behavioral activation (not traditional marketing)
Journey: **Signed Agreement → First Placement → Successful Recovery → Long-Term Client.**
Every creative decision supports this progression.

## Marketing Philosophy — Pattern Interrupt (Disruptive)
- First 3 seconds of every video must surprise the viewer.
- Humor creates attention; SWRS provides the solution.
- Viewer finishes every episode feeling working with SWRS is easy.
- **Never portray SWRS as difficult. The frustration belongs to the situation — not to SWRS.**

## Brand Personality
Professional · Helpful · Confident · Approachable · Creative.
**Never sarcastic. Never negative. Never make clients feel foolish.**

## Campaign Theme (recurring promise)
**"Getting your accounts to SWRS is easier than you think."**

## Campaign Structure — every episode (20–30s)
1. Unexpected hook
2. Relatable frustration
3. SWRS simplifies the problem
4. Clear call to action
5. Brand close

## Primary Call to Action
Today: **Email your placements to info@swrecovery.com. We'll take care of the rest.**
Future options (emphasize flexibility, don't replace the portal): secure portal, secure
email, Excel upload, account-manager assistance.

## Recurring Character — Victor the Vampire
**Purpose:** Victor represents every busy business owner.
**Characteristics:** professional office worker; dramatic; easily frustrated; friendly;
loves cash flow; hates unnecessary complications; **never blames SWRS**; learns an easier
way every episode.
**Recurring visual gags:** turns into a bat; turns into dust; keyboard smoke; flying
paperwork; coffee spills; printer chaos; comic "poof" effects.
Victor is the recognizable face of the campaign.

## Additional Disruptive Concepts (future episodes / seasonal)
- **Mission Impossible** — impossible "mission" to submit accounts; everything goes wrong; "It doesn't have to be this difficult."
- **Jurassic Accounts** — old receivables become a dinosaur of unpaid invoices; prompt placement shrinks the monster.
- **Time Traveler** — client sees a future office buried in overdue invoices; rewinds; emails SWRS immediately.
- **Escape Room** — client trapped solving login puzzles; hidden exit = email your accounts to SWRS.

## Production Standards
- Max length **30s**; opening hook within **3s**.
- **Caption every video.**
- **Always** include: logo animation · music · sound effects · final CTA.

## Deliverables Per Episode
Creative Brief · Objective · Audience · Pain Point · Desired Action · Storyboard · Shot
List · Camera Angles · Lighting · Scene Timing · Voiceover · Closed Captions · AI Prompt
Package · Motion Graphics Notes · Sound Design · Export Files.

## Video Specifications
- **16:9** — email campaigns
- **1:1** — LinkedIn
- **9:16** — short-form video
- **1080p** delivery; **4K** master archive

## AI Workflow
Campaign Planning → Storyboard Development → Prompt Generation → AI Video Generation →
Editing → Branding → Approval → Distribution → Performance Reporting.

## AI Tools (preferred)
Google Veo · Runway · Kling · Pika · Adobe Premiere · After Effects · Creatomate · Claude · OpenAI.

## Orchestration ("Higgins"/Claude Integration)
Orchestrator should handle: episode planning · storyboard creation · prompt generation ·
voiceover generation · email generation · approval routing · production tracking · version
history · asset organization · campaign management · video status · metrics. If it supports
API calls + webhooks, it orchestrates the workflow while dedicated AI video tools render.

## CRM Integration (behavioral triggers)
Send onboarding videos based on behavior: signed contract; no first placement after 7 / 14
/ 30 / 45 / 60 days. **Stop the sequence once the first placement is received.**

## Email Campaign (per episode)
Subject Line · Preview Text · Email Copy · Embedded Video · Call to Action · Reply Option · Tracking Links.

## Analytics Dashboard
Contract Date · First Placement Date · Days Until First Placement · Video Views · Video
Completion Rate · Portal Logins · Email Replies · Email Placements · Revenue Generated ·
Placement Percentage · Activation Rate · Episode Performance · A/B Test Results.

## Creative Direction
Humor lowers anxiety. Message is always **SWRS makes it easy.** Clients never feel
embarrassed. Build confidence while remaining memorable.

## Success Criteria
Primary KPI: increase first-placement rate. Secondary: reduce time to first placement;
increase engagement; increase accounts submitted; increase placement revenue.

## Phase 1 Deliverables
1. Finalize creative direction. 2. Design Victor the Vampire. 3. Produce Episode 1.
4. Test internally. 5. Gather feedback. 6. Revise. 7. Produce remaining 11 episodes.
8. Build campaign automation. 9. Launch to new clients. 10. Measure activation for 90 days.

## Vendor Requirements
Experience with: AI-generated commercial video · character consistency · storyboarding ·
motion graphics · AI prompting · branded marketing campaigns · multi-format exports ·
editable project delivery.

## Final Vision
A repeatable production system — not a one-time project. Quickly create consistent, branded
onboarding content that reduces client friction, accelerates first account placements, and
supports measurable business growth. Every new episode easy to produce by following the
established creative standards, character guide, storyboard format, and production workflow.
