## Logo rules (Brand Book pp. 4–6)

- Do NOT rotate, distort, recolor, crop/reproduce incompletely, or add borders/strokes.
- Respect the protection zone (clear space) around the logo — nothing intrudes.
- Minimum width: **100 px on screen**, 39 mm in print.
- In video: white logo over navy/dark scenes, soft drop shadow permitted for legibility
  (shadow is a legibility aid behind the logo, not a stroke on it).
- **Placement rule (client directive, 2026-08-04): the logo is only to be used in the
  last scene** — the brand-close card. Never overlay it on story footage.

## Voice (Brand Book + Production Handoff)

Professional · Helpful · Confident · Approachable · Creative.
Never sarcastic, never negative, never make clients feel foolish.
The frustration always belongs to the situation — never to SWRS.
